Lea Jordan

PhD Candidate
Department of Sociology, University of Zurich

I am a PhD Candidate in the Department of Sociology at the University of Zurich. My PhD is funded by Human Reproduction Reloaded (H2R) , an interdisciplinary research program at the University of Zurich. As part of H2R, I work within the research team of Human Reproduction in Societies and Markets .

My research interests lie at the intersection of gender inequality, work and family, and social stratification. I study how gender inequalities emerge and evolve across the life course, particularly in relation to parenthood, employment, and career decisions. I am especially interested in how gender norms, workplace structures, and institutional contexts shape these inequalities. In my work, I use both observational and experimental methods.
